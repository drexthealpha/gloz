The one message that matters, answered from your glasses with one tap.

Gloz shows the messages from the Telegram, Slack and Discord chats you pick on your Even G2, one at a time, the most pressing first: who wrote, one line of what they want, and a reply already drafted by AI in their language, with its meaning shown in yours.

One tap on the glasses or the R1 ring sends it, with 2 seconds to take it back. Double tap for another of three replies; swipe to skip. Hold and say a few words to have the reply rewritten. Nothing is sent without your tap or your word, and replies wait through a lost signal.

Ten minutes before your next Google Calendar meeting, a card shows who and when, with "On my way." ready to send in the chat where that person last wrote. Hold on "No new messages" and ask what someone said: a one-line answer from your chats.

Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Thai, Hebrew and other scripts the glasses cannot show on their own are drawn as images, at the size you pick. Quiet hours keep messages waiting until the hour you choose.

Your keys and your message text stay on your own Gloz server; message text is dropped once the reply is sent. "Delete everything" on the phone forgets it all.

Free and open source (MIT).
